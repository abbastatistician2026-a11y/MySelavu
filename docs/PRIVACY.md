# MySelavu privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

MySelavu is a personal salary, budget and expense tracker.

- **No data collection.** MySelavu does not collect, send or share any personal or financial data.
- **No internet.** The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to any server.
- **No accounts, ads or analytics.**
- **Your data stays on your phone**, in the app's private storage.
- **Backups are your choice.** If you save a backup file, you choose where it goes (for example your own
  Google Drive) using Android's standard "Save" screen. Backups can be locked with a password
  (AES-256 encryption); without the password nobody, including the developer, can read them.
- **Android Auto Backup:** if it is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's database in
  your personal Google account backup, as it does for other apps. This is managed by Android, not by MySelavu.
- **Deleting data:** you can clear old history or delete all data at any time in *Menu → Backup & storage*.
  Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the phone.
